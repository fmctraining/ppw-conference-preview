Option 1 — bare wordmark

Logo sits directly on the photo, top-right. Lightest touch. Watch how it holds up on the brighter photos (London especially).

Option 6 — wordmark on a frosted plate

Same logo and position, on a subtle glass panel so it stays crisp regardless of what's behind it.

My recommendation: Option 6. These wordmarks are full-colour with a multi-colour ring, so on a busy or bright photo (London's sunset, Vegas' neon) the bare version loses definition fast. The plate keeps every city consistent and legible. Option 1 is cleaner but only safe if every tile photo stays dark and simple.
